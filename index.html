THE PROBLEM
Farmers don't lose money growing crops.
They lose it selling them.

I visited farms across Odisha and Jharkhand. Sat with farmers. Asked where they sell, how much profit they make, whether they know a better price exists nearby.

Almost every farmer sells locally by default — not because it's best. Because they have no easy way to compare.

Every harvest season, the same question came up at home — where should we sell? Different mandis offered different prices, but comparing them was difficult. AgroPredict started as an attempt to make that decision easier for my family and nearby farmers.

WHAT I LEARNED

What Farmers Taught Me

Key insights from conversations — not assumptions, real observations.

💰
Profit matters more than priceFarmers care about take-home after transport — not just the mandi rate on a board.
🌾
Variety matters more than crop name"Rice" is not enough — Basmati, Sona Masuri, Common are completely different decisions.
🔊
Voice beats typingMany farmers prefer to speak the crop name rather than type — especially in Hindi.
📊
Information creates profitKnowing one better mandi exists can change the selling decision entirely.
🤝
Buyer discovery is harder than price discoveryFinding who is buying at a good rate is often the bigger problem.
🏘️
Small farmers lack market connectionsLarge farmers have agents. Small farmers rely on whoever shows up first.

AgroPredict
AGRITECH · FLUTTER · AGMARKNET API · GPS · HINDI + VOICE · FIELD TESTED

A crop price decision-support app that helps farmers decide where and when to sell for better profit. Connects to India's AGMARKNET government data. Full Hindi interface. Built from real farmer feedback across Odisha and Jharkhand.

WHAT IT DOES
GPS MANDINearest mandi with approximate distance and travel time
PROFIT CALCNet profit after estimated transport cost — before the trip
SELL / WAITRule-based advice with plain reasoning — no ML model
VOICE OUTPUTReads advice aloud in Hindi — farmer hears without reading
VARIETIESBasmati vs Common — because farmers think in varieties
HINDI UIFull Hindi interface + ₹/quintal ↔ ₹/kg toggle
