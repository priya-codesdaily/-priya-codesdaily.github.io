03 About

More than my job title

I'm a BCA student and software developer who likes learning by building. My path wasn't a straight line — I moved from short-term work and customer support to teaching, then eventually found my way into software development. Each stage gave me something I still use today.

👩‍🏫

Teaching

Teaching taught me to simplify.

Working with children taught me that knowing something isn't enough — you need to communicate it.

🌾

Farming

Growing up around farming made real-world problems impossible to ignore.

It shaped the kind of software I want to build — practical tools that can actually help someone outside a tech bubble.

💻

Building

Building gives me the freedom to turn questions into experiments.

I don't want to build projects just to fill a portfolio. I want to understand problems deeply enough to build something useful.
