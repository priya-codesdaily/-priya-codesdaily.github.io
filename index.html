MY JOURNEY

Drag sideways, or tap a stop.

I finished school without really knowing what I wanted to become. No clear roadmap, no one telling me which path to take. So I started experimenting.

I was introduced to Aapna through people I met while still figuring out what to do. I explored affiliate marketing and later worked around KYC and customer support — short-term and target-driven, but it taught me that work is different when someone depends on you to deliver.

I travelled to Tamil Nadu after a referral and completed training before working across two schools — Vivek Ananda School and Kalvi Karpagam School. It wasn't where I expected my career to go, but it taught me confidence, responsibility and how to work with people.
