Every harvest season, the same question came up at home — where should we sell? Different mandis offered different prices, but comparing them was difficult. AgroPredict started as an attempt to make that decision easier for my family and nearby farmers.

BUILT FROM

✓ Family farming experience — Jharkhand–Odisha border

01 — THE PROBLEM

Farmers don't lose money growing crops.
They lose it selling them.

I visited farms across Odisha and Jharkhand. Sat with farmers. Asked where they sell, how much profit they make, whether they know a better price exists nearby. Almost every farmer sells locally by default — not because it's best. Because they have no way to compare.

→ Crop variety selector added in 24 hours

03 — PROJECTS

What I Built

AgroPredict
AGRITECH · FLUTTER · AGMARKNET API · GPS · HINDI + VOICE

A crop price decision-support app that helps farmers decide where and when to sell for better profit. Connects to India's AGMARKNET government data. Full Hindi interface. Tested with farmers across Odisha and Jharkhand.

WHAT IT DOES
GPS MANDINearest mandi with approximate distance and travel time
PROFIT CALCNet profit after estimated transport cost before the trip
SELL / WAITRule-based advice with plain reasoning — no ML model
VOICE OUTPUTReads advice aloud in Hindi — farmer hears without reading
VARIETIESBasmati vs Common — farmers think in varieties
HINDI UIFull Hindi interface + ₹/quintal ↔ ₹/kg toggle
